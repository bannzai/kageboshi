# Privacy Policy

bannzai ("we" or "the Provider") sets out this Privacy Policy ("this Policy") for how we handle user information, including personal information, in the game "Kageboshi" ("the Game").

## Information we collect and how

### Game data (stored only on your device)

The Game's settings and progress are **stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers**. The Game does not communicate with any server operated by us and does not require an account. The Game does not include any third-party service for advertising or usage analytics.

### Information handled by Steam

The Game is distributed through Steam, operated by Valve Corporation. Purchases and payments, your Steam account, and Steam features (library, achievements, cloud saves, etc.) are handled by Valve Corporation under its Privacy Policy ( https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/ ). We do not receive payment information such as credit card details. Through Steamworks we can only see aggregated, non-identifying figures such as sales and wishlist counts.

### Information we collect

Only when you contact us by email or other means do we receive your contact details and the content of your inquiry.

## Purposes of use

- To respond to your inquiries and bug reports about the Game
- To provide, maintain, protect, and improve the Game
- To notify you of changes to the terms and policies of the Game

## Retention period

Contact details and inquiry contents are deleted one year after we finish handling the inquiry, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

## Disclosure to third parties

We do not provide personal information to third parties (including those outside Japan) without your prior consent, except in the following cases:

- When we outsource all or part of the handling of personal information within the scope necessary to achieve the purposes of use
- When personal information is transferred due to a merger or other succession of business
- When cooperation is required with a national or local government agency, or a party entrusted by it, performing duties prescribed by law, and obtaining your consent could interfere with those duties
- Other cases permitted by the Act on the Protection of Personal Information or other laws

## Disclosure, correction, suspension of use, and deletion

When you request disclosure, correction, suspension of use, or deletion of your personal information under the Act on the Protection of Personal Information, we will respond without delay after confirming that the request comes from you (if no such personal information exists, we will notify you of that). This does not apply where the law does not impose such obligations on us. Please send requests by email to the contact below.

Game data stored only on your device is not held by us, so you can delete it yourself by uninstalling the Game or deleting its save folder. For information related to your Steam account, please contact Valve Corporation.
